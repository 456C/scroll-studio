# Scroll Studio icon

Generated November 1, 2026 using the built-in imagegen tool. The first concept, originally approved in chat, remains at `assets/scroll-studio-icon-concept.png`. After feedback about small-size legibility, the replacement is `assets/scroll-studio-icon-concept-v2.png`: a stronger blue S silhouette with a flat graphite tile. The large toolbar icon is removed at the user's request. The window and Windows executable retain the small app identity.

The runtime uses a 256 px PNG; Windows Explorer uses a seven-size ICO embedded at build time. `scripts/prepare-icon.ps1` reproduces the size/format conversions from v2 while preserving transparency. Full concepts are not embedded in the executable. No CLI/API key path was used.

## Original prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: production desktop application icon for Scroll Studio, a minimalist native webtoon comic creation app. Create ONE polished icon only, square 1024 by 1024 composition on a genuinely transparent outside background. Center a large rounded-square graphite tile with soft continuous corners, occupying about 92 percent of the image. Inside it a bold, distinctive icy-blue vertical scrolling comic ribbon: three broad stacked comic panels with asymmetric lengths and a gentle curling lower edge, together suggesting an elegant S and a downward reading flow. The blue scrolling-panel symbol must remain unmistakably legible at 24 pixels. Premium restrained liquid glass material, subtle translucent blue depth and a delicate cool rim highlight, tactile but nearly frontal and flat, no perspective tilt. Graphite charcoal #202124 tile, pale sky blue #7AB8F5 symbol, a hint of cyan, high contrast, carefully controlled soft reflections. One strong silhouette, few simple shapes, calm professional creative software aesthetic. No letters, no words, no typography, no mockup, no device, no extra objects, no multi-icon grid, no dramatic background, no floating shadow outside the tile. Preserve actual alpha outside the rounded tile.

## Simplification prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Edit target: the attached current Scroll Studio app icon. Redesign it completely for excellent readability at 16, 24, and 32 pixels. Keep the blue and graphite identity and the idea of a vertical comic scroll, but simplify to one very bold, instantly recognizable geometric S-shaped scroll mark. A solid pale electric-blue S ribbon with just two broad bends, wide negative spaces, no small panel seams or tiny curled tube. Center it large, filling roughly 70 percent of a dark graphite rounded square tile. Flat vector-like graphic, front-on orthographic, crisp clean edges, balanced generous silhouette, refined professional creative-software identity. The tile has a simple solid charcoal fill and smoothly rounded corners, no visible border. The S is the only symbol; no other letters or words. Remove ALL 3D bevels, reflections, metallic edging, glass highlights, wispy glow, fine texture, perspective, micro-details, cast shadow, and decorative chrome. High contrast. Output exactly one finished square app icon, tightly framed with tile covering almost the entire image, transparent pixels only outside the tile corners. No mockup, no presentation sheet, no labels, no multiple variants, no surrounding background.
