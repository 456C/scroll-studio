# Scroll Studio: free downloads, optional support

Research checked November 1, 2026. Prices below are published USD rates, not quotes for a particular country or account. This document recommends a model; no payment account, checkout or paid service has been created.

## Recommendation

Keep **Download for Windows** as a direct, free download. Add one quiet **Support 456C** link to an optional Ko-fi page after its owner has configured and verified it. Downloading and using the full desktop application should require no signup, payment, email address, advertisement or bundled installer offer.

Free downloads do not generate money automatically. Revenue would come from people voluntarily supporting development, purchasing optional content, or later paying for a separate publishing service. Download counts measure reach; they are not payable revenue.

Start with one-time support rather than a membership that commits the developer to recurring rewards. Ko-fi Free currently takes **0% platform fees on one-time tips**, with normal PayPal/Stripe processing fees still payable. Its Standard mode takes 5%, and new creators start with that mode enabled, so confirm the selected mode before advertising the fee. Monthly tips, memberships and shop sales have a 5% platform fee on the Free plan. [Ko-fi fee schedule](https://help.ko-fi.com/hc/en-us/articles/360002506494-Does-Ko-fi-take-a-fee)

Supporters do not need a Ko-fi account, although the payment flow collects their email and payment details. This requirement belongs only to optional support, never to the app download. [Ko-fi supporter information](https://help.ko-fi.com/hc/en-us/articles/360009392953-What-information-do-supporters-share-Public-private-options)

Use the detailed fee pages rather than assuming headline rates include every payment and payout cost. Gumroad currently applies a lower direct-sale tier after $20,000 paid sales within a calendar month; that is not needed for the launch decision. [Gumroad fee tiers](https://gumroad.com/help/article/66-gumroads-fees)

Ko-fi is not merchant of record and does not remit the creator's transaction taxes. itch.io's collected-payout model makes itch.io merchant of record and handles VAT, while its direct-payment model leaves those responsibilities with the seller. Gumroad and Lemon Squeezy offer merchant-of-record checkout tax handling. That handling is not a promise to cover the developer's income taxes or every business obligation. [Ko-fi tax model](https://help.ko-fi.com/hc/en-us/articles/10792069957661-How-tax-works-on-Ko-fi), [itch.io payment modes](https://itch.io/docs/creators/payments), [Gumroad pricing](https://gumroad.com/pricing), [Lemon Squeezy pricing](https://www.lemonsqueezy.com/pricing)

## Minimal placement

The main download area should contain:

- **Download for Windows** — prominent primary action.
- Version and architecture in small metadata; portable ZIP as a secondary download.
- **Free. No ads.** — short confirmation.
- **Support 456C** — a quiet text link below the download area or in the footer.

The support link opens the chosen provider only when clicked. Do not load a payment widget, display a donation modal, delay downloads, preselect a recurring payment, or show a fake download button. Keep optional support out of the startup splash and routine canvas work. An About link in the app is sufficient if added later.

Until an owner-controlled support URL exists and is verified, leave the public link absent. Do not guess a username, publish a placeholder payment destination or claim that support is already available.

An itch.io mirror could use `$0 or Donate` with the identical installer and checksums. Its skip-payment option is documented; the main site remains the shortest route. itch.io says it does not place ads on creator pages or modify uploaded downloads. [Pricing](https://itch.io/docs/creators/pricing), [creator FAQ](https://itch.io/docs/creators/faq)

## Optional products and future revenue

After the free release is stable, test one useful optional pack: story starter templates, a cohesive bubble preset collection, or an original reference/background kit. Sell reusable content rather than access to existing editing tools. Users must remain able to create their own equivalents in the free app. Only include artwork, fonts and assets whose redistribution rights cover the pack.

For paid packs, start with provider-hosted checkout and delivery. Lemon Squeezy is the initial candidate when its account/product eligibility and payout country fit; compare actual fee totals with itch.io before choosing. Neither service is assumed to approve an account automatically. Avoid implementing licenses or a desktop account system merely to sell files.

The later webtoon website can earn from paid chapter unlocks, an explicitly disclosed share of creator sales, and optional hosted publishing services. Those are separate products: the desktop editor and its local `.scro` workflow remain free. Begin with an upload/reader pilot before adding coins, payouts or subscriptions; the transactional ledger and payment-provider eligibility work is described in [the hosting plan](hosting-research.md).

Measure optional support and pack sales after launch before promising income. Net revenue depends on voluntary conversion, price, payment fees, refunds, operating costs and applicable taxes. No minimum earnings or per-download payout is implied.
