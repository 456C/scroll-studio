Your story stays with you.

The desktop app

Scroll Studio saves your work on your computer. This release does not require an account, upload your artwork, include advertising, or send usage analytics. Image generation happens in the separate service you choose.

This download site

The page does not use advertising, analytics scripts, or tracking cookies. The hosting provider processes ordinary network requests to deliver the page and downloads. A private preview may require its hosting provider’s sign-in.

The browser Assets editor

Opening a .scro file or importing a reference sends it to this server for editing. Workspaces are private to your browser session and expire after 24 hours without use. An essential session cookie keeps each visitor’s work separate. Original images are preserved; smaller previews are generated for browsing.

A recovery draft containing world details is also stored in this browser. You can remove it in the editor’s Settings. Export .scro to keep your complete world and transfer it to the desktop app. This is temporary editing, not a permanent cloud backup. No analytics, advertising, or payment is required.

Future services

Chapter uploads, payments, and reader accounts are not part of this site. If those services launch, they will have their own clear privacy information.
